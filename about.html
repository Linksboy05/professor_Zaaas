Dean

All About the Professor

Dean Litster, aka. Professor Zaaa, is known for his art of pizza making. He has competed in competitions around the world, including Canada and Las Vegas, with his amazing creations. As a young boy, Dean dreamed of being an astronaut. He later came to love the art of television broadcasting, where he went on to school at Specs Howard, to learn about how to make everything look the best on screen. That is where he came across the difficult decision: make pizzas or become a television slave. Today, Dean has taken his passion 25 years into the future. He has mixed up the pizza game and created pizzas the average person would have never thought possible. He is consistently pushing boundaries in the pursuit of the perfect zaaa. Winning awards such as number 1 in Canada and multiple top 5 in the world, even placing number 1 in the pizza box folding competition, makes him a force to be reckoned with. He is always looking for new inspiration in everything he does! Professor zaaa has become just that, a professor, a mentor, a legend of his craft. Just try it for yourself and find out what sets him apart… or better yet… find out… the Prof Zaaa Difference

Lisa

All About His Deputy

Lisa Bottone, also known as Sergaent Zaaa, got her start at Fanshawe College in London, Ontario. That is where she studied Hospitality Management and fell in love with the restaurant industry. She proceeded to receive her diploma with a 4.2 GPA and the only person in her class to achieve the presidents honour roll. Her love of food and service led her to a life in the hospitality industry, with a passion for serving people. Lisa’s passion for the Hospitality Industry led her to push herself in all aspects, she expanded her cooking knowledge while continuing to make long lasting “regulars” in every job she held. To date, Lisa has 20 years experience in the hospitality industry. She first met Dean in 2020 when she became his pizza stretcher. Lisa continued to work along side Dean, solely stretching pizzas, for 3 years. Dean would say to her, “you need to know how to make a beautiful canvas before you paint your picture”. These words resonated with her and pushed her even further. Lisa is now competing in pizza competitions along side The Professor and have received multiple top 20 positions in Canada with her “out of the box” creations. The sky’s the limit with her, so let’s see how far she soars!
